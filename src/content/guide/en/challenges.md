## Earning Stars

Stars are the premium currency. They are earned in the game:

| Source | Stars |
|---|---|
| Daily tasks | 8 a game day, 168 a season |
| Season tasks | 100 a season |
| Lifetime achievements | a one-time reward for each of 5 levels in 25 tracks |
| Rapid league | 5, 3, 2 or 1 for places one to four; up to 10 entries a day |

**Daily tasks** count only the current game day and start over with the next one. **Season tasks**
add up every day of the season. Both must be claimed before their day or season ends.

**Achievements** never reset - not between levels, seasons or replacement clubs. Only completed
actions count. Sporting achievements use official first-team league and Elite games, including the
playoff; farm, Rapid and forfeited games do not count.

## Spending Stars

Stars, or real money, buy:

- cosmetic changes - a new name or nickname, club colours, a logo,
- the **Ads Pass** for 21 days, which removes ads but does not change any daily limits,
- the **Sponsor Pass** for 21 days, which raises sponsor income in Flow and gold pucks by 20 %.

Players, Flow and gold pucks cannot be bought with money. The Sponsor Pass is a real economic
advantage: renewable Star income alone is not enough to keep it active every season.
