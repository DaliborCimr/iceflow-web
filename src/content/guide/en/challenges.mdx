Stars are the premium currency. They are earned by playing; they can also be bought.

## Earning Stars

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="today" title="Daily tasks: 8 a day">
    168 a season. They count only the current game day and start over with the next one - claim them
    before the day ends.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="date_range" title="Season tasks: 100 a season">
    They add up every day of the season - claim them before it ends.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="military_tech" title="Lifetime achievements">
    25 tracks with 5 levels each and a one-time reward per level. Progress never resets - not between
    levels, seasons or replacement clubs.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="bolt" title="Rapid league: 5, 3, 2 or 1">
    For places one to four, up to ten entries a day. See [Season and competitions](../season/).
  </Rule>
</Rules>

<Callout type="note" title="What counts for achievements">
  Only completed actions. Sporting achievements use official first-team league and Elite games,
  including the playoff; farm, Rapid and forfeited games do not count.
</Callout>

## Spending Stars

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="palette" title="Cosmetics">
    A new name or nickname, club colours, a logo.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="block" title="Ads Pass - 21 days">
    Removes ads. Daily limits stay the same.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="trending_up" title="Sponsor Pass - 21 days">
    Raises sponsor income in Flow and gold pucks by 20 %.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## What money can and cannot buy

Players, Flow and gold pucks cannot be bought with money. Stars and the items above can.

The Sponsor Pass is a real economic advantage: renewable Star income alone is not enough to keep it
active every season. Ice Flow is therefore not pay-to-win in the sense of buying players or strength,
but paying does give a moderate long-term economic benefit.
