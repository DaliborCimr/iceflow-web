Stars are the premium currency earned by playing. Real-money payments are not enabled in the
current app.

## Earning Stars

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="today" title="Daily tasks: 10 a day">
    Up to 210 a season: six tasks for 1 or 2 stars each, and 2 more for completing all of them. They count only the current game day and start over with the next one - claim them
    before the day ends.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="date_range" title="Season tasks: 130 a season">
    Six tasks for 10 to 25 stars each, and 30 more for completing all of them. They add up every day of the season - claim them before it ends.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="done_all" title="Collect at once">
    Claiming one finished task collects every finished task of the same period - daily or season -
    including the bonus for completing all of them.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="military_tech" title="Lifetime achievements">
    25 tracks with 5 levels each and a one-time reward per level. Progress never resets - not between
    levels, seasons or replacement clubs.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="bolt" title="Rapid league: 5, 3, 2 or 1">
    For places one to four, up to ten entries a day. See [Season and competitions](../season/).
  </Rule>
</Rules>

<Callout type="note" title="What counts for achievements">
  Only completed actions. Sporting achievements use official first-team league and Elite games,
  including the playoff; farm, Rapid and forfeited games do not count. A team
  training session that expires unused counts for no task or achievement.
</Callout>

Home’s Challenges tile shows daily and season tasks and the number of Rapid league entries left
today. This is the remaining daily allowance, not the separate free ticket returned when an entry
leaves the draw before your own match.

**Double Crown** tracks seasons with at least two of the three trophies; **Clean Sweep** tracks
seasons with all three. They are the regular-league group stage, league playoff and Elite Series
playoff. A Clean Sweep also counts towards Double Crown; Rapid wins are separate.

## GSAA board and Golden Glove

GSAA - goals saved above average - compares a goalie with the league's average goalie on the same
shots. The league's GSAA board shows the top 25. During the group stage a goalie counts for the
Golden Glove achievement only once he has played at least 15/44 of his team's games so far, rounded
up - 15 of 44 by the end. Goalies below that keep their rank but are struck through. In the playoff
one game is enough; Elite has no limit, since it gives no award.

<Callout type="note" title="Why 15 of 44">
  The NHL's William M. Jennings Trophy needs 25 of 82 games, about 30 %
  ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_M._Jennings_Trophy)). Ice Flow asks a little
  more of a 44-game group stage, and counts it as the season goes so that the board means something
  from the first weeks.
</Callout>

## Spending Stars

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="palette" title="Name and colours">
    A new club name or nickname, or new club colours - 100 stars each, for Stars only.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="image" title="Logo">
    A basic logo for 300 stars, a premium one for 500.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="block" title="Ads Pass - 21 days">
    Removes ads. Daily limits stay the same.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="trending_up" title="Sponsor Pass - 21 days">
    Raises sponsor income in Flow and gold pucks by 20 %.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## What money can and cannot buy

Players, Flow and gold pucks cannot be bought with money. Logos and both passes are currently
available for Stars; a new name and new colours also use Stars. Real-money purchases and
**Support the game** are planned, not available in the current app. <TestOnly />
