## Two currencies

- **Flow** (cash) pays salaries and facilities.
- **Gold pucks** are the currency of the transfer market, scouting and junior signings.

## Arena and staff

The **arena** is made up of stands, VIP, fan shop, food, parking and training facilities. They bring
matchday income and fans; training facilities also help development. The arena as a whole goes up a
level only once all its parts have reached that level.

The **staff** are coaching, medical, amateur scouting, professional scouting, front office and player
development. Each improves its own part of the club - training, recovery and injuries, junior
reports, match preparation, contracts and sponsor offers, and development in farm games.

- One upgrade at a time in the arena group and one in the staff group. Higher levels take longer -
  from an hour of game time to several game days. No facility may get more than two levels ahead of
  the rest of its group.
- Arena facilities wear every day, higher levels faster, and a worn facility gives less than its
  level promises. Automatic maintenance keeps each at 75 % condition; a repair brings it to 100 %,
  and an upgrade starts only from a repaired facility.

## Income and expenses

- **Income.** Sponsors pay fixed instalments on days 1, 8 and 15. Every home game brings matchday
  income from tickets, VIP, fan shop, food and parking - more for a strong, winning team, a big
  fanbase and the playoff.
- **Expenses.** Player and staff salaries are paid every day; repairs and upgrades when you order
  them.
- **Running short.** The club warns you when money will not last until the next sponsor payment. If
  cash goes below zero, the club sells its highest earner it can spare, one a day, until it is back
  above zero.

## Sponsors

Each season you sign one cash sponsor and one gold-puck sponsor; the choice holds until the next
season. Offers grow with the team's strength, the club's reputation and the front office. There are
three kinds:

| Sponsor | Pays |
|---|---|
| Steady / TV rights | the whole contract in fixed instalments, whatever happens |
| Administration | smaller instalments plus a reward for every game day you open the game |
| Match presence | smaller instalments plus a payment for every league game, with the playoff as extra upside |

## Reputation and fans

At the end of every season reputation and fans grow with a winning record and shrink with a losing
one. They fill the arena and raise sponsor offers. A high finish and a long playoff run raise next
season's offers by up to 10 %.
