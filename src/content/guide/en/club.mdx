A strong team needs a club that can afford it. The arena earns money, the staff make the team
better, and both cost money to build and keep up.

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="stadium" title="Arena">
    Stands, VIP, fan shop, food, parking and training facilities. They bring matchday income and
    fans; training facilities also help development. The arena as a whole goes up a level only once
    all its parts have reached it.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="badge" title="Staff">
    Coaching, medical, amateur scouting, professional scouting, front office and player development.
    Each improves its own part of the club - training, recovery and injuries, junior reports, match
    preparation, contracts and sponsor offers, and development in farm games.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="construction" title="Upgrades">
    One at a time in the arena and one in the staff. Higher levels take longer - an hour of game time
    for level 2, a game day for level 3 and a day more for each level after, up to eight game days for
    level 10. No facility may get more than two levels ahead of the rest of its
    group.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="build" title="Condition">
    Arena facilities wear every day, higher levels faster, and a worn facility gives less than its
    level promises. Automatic maintenance keeps each at 75 %; a repair brings it to 100 %, and an
    upgrade starts only from a repaired facility. Staff do not wear and need no repairs.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Money in, money out

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="savings" title="Income">
    Sponsors pay fixed instalments on days 1, 8 and 15. Every home game brings matchday income from
    tickets, VIP, fan shop, food and parking - more for a strong, winning team, a big fanbase and the
    playoff.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="receipt_long" title="Expenses">
    Player and staff salaries are paid every day; repairs and upgrades when you order them.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="warning" title="Running short">
    The club warns about its budget outlook, taking account of income, salaries, maintenance
    and a reserve for expiring contracts. If cash goes below zero, the club sells the highest earner it can spare, one a day, until it is back above zero.
    Such a forced sale pays no buyout, but the sale price is only half of the player's reference value
    (instead of 70 %), and the club receives its usual 75 % share of that.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

The finance overview shows today and the season total separately for Flow and gold pucks. A row
is hidden only when all its amounts in both currencies are zero. A row with season activity stays
visible even when today is zero. Transfer purchases count completed deals, not temporary bid holds.
Contract signing and extension bonuses are included with player pay.

The budget’s signing-bonus reserve covers active contracts with at most one season left, excluding
players with announced retirement. It does not reserve a signing bonus for longer contracts.

## Club history

The trophy counters distinguish league playoff titles, first places in the regular season and
Elite Series titles. Each season’s row also distinguishes its regular-season rank from its playoff
finish; winning the group stage is not the same as winning the league playoff.

## When you are away

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="ac_unit" title="Frozen club">
    A club whose manager has not opened the game for 18 game days is frozen. It stays in its league
    and plays its games with the lineup it was left with; fatigue recovers and injuries heal. Nothing
    else moves: no salaries, income, sponsor payments, training, scouting, ageing or contracts.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="login" title="Coming back">
    Signing in or opening the app unfreezes the club as you left it. At the change of season most
    frozen clubs leave the competition to make room; such a club returns into the running season as
    soon as its manager is back.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Sponsors

Each season you sign **one cash sponsor and one gold-puck sponsor**; the choice holds until the next
season. Offers grow with your team's strength, your reputation and your front office.

| Sponsor | How it pays |
|---|---|
| Safe sponsor / TV rights | the whole contract in fixed instalments, whatever happens |
| Administration | smaller instalments plus a reward for every game day you open the game |
| Match presence | smaller instalments plus a payment for every league game - group stage and playoff - in which you were in its live match - during play, the warm-up or an intermission; opening the app alone is not enough |

## Reputation and fans

At the end of every season reputation and fans grow with a winning record and shrink with a losing
one. They fill the arena and raise sponsor offers. A high finish and a long playoff run raise next
season's offers by up to 10 %. Winning the league playoff also gives reputation and fans a small
extra boost, and qualifying for Elite a smaller one; only the best result of the season counts.
