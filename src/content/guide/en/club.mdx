A strong team needs a club that can afford it. The arena earns money, the staff make the team
better, and both cost money to build and keep up.

## Two currencies

<Rules columns>
  <Rule icon="payments" title="Flow (cash)">
    Pays salaries, staff and facilities.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="toll" title="Gold pucks">
    The currency of the transfer market, scouting and junior signings.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Arena and staff

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="stadium" title="Arena">
    Stands, VIP, fan shop, food, parking and training facilities. They bring matchday income and
    fans; training facilities also help development. The arena as a whole goes up a level only once
    all its parts have reached it.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="badge" title="Staff">
    Coaching, medical, amateur scouting, professional scouting, front office and player development.
    Each improves its own part of the club - training, recovery and injuries, junior reports, match
    preparation, contracts and sponsor offers, and development in farm games.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="construction" title="Upgrades">
    One at a time in the arena and one in the staff. Higher levels take longer - from an hour of game
    time to several game days. No facility may get more than two levels ahead of the rest of its
    group.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="build" title="Condition">
    Arena facilities wear every day, higher levels faster, and a worn facility gives less than its
    level promises. Automatic maintenance keeps each at 75 %; a repair brings it to 100 %, and an
    upgrade starts only from a repaired facility.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Money in, money out

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="savings" title="Income">
    Sponsors pay fixed instalments on days 1, 8 and 15. Every home game brings matchday income from
    tickets, VIP, fan shop, food and parking - more for a strong, winning team, a big fanbase and the
    playoff.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="receipt_long" title="Expenses">
    Player and staff salaries are paid every day; repairs and upgrades when you order them.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="warning" title="Running short">
    The club warns you when money will not last until the next sponsor payment. If cash goes below
    zero, the club sells the highest earner it can spare, one a day, until it is back above zero.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Sponsors

Each season you sign **one cash sponsor and one gold-puck sponsor**; the choice holds until the next
season. Offers grow with your team's strength, your reputation and your front office.

| Sponsor | How it pays |
|---|---|
| Safe sponsor / TV rights | the whole contract in fixed instalments, whatever happens |
| Administration | smaller instalments plus a reward for every game day you open the game |
| Match presence | smaller instalments plus a payment for every league game, more in the playoff |

## Reputation and fans

At the end of every season reputation and fans grow with a winning record and shrink with a losing
one. They fill the arena and raise sponsor offers. A high finish and a long playoff run raise next
season's offers by up to 10 %.
