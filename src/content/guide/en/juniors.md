## Two seasons per junior

Your scouts find fifteen prospects every season. They are seventeen: this season you only scout
them. At eighteen they move to **To sign** and can be signed early in the next season.

## Scouting reports

A prospect's abilities and traits are shown as ranges. They narrow in two ways:

- **by themselves**, a little every day of the season,
- **with a report** bought with gold pucks - Basic narrows a little, Advanced more, Deep the most.
  Better amateur scouting staff make every report sharper.

A bought report cannot narrow below a floor, and the floor is lower the fewer days of the season are
left. At eighteen the range is fixed at what was reached at the end of the scouting season; waiting
during the signing window does not improve it.

The traits of a junior are summarised as **TRT** - talent, professionalism, injury resistance,
discipline and mentality in one estimate, with talent and professionalism weighing the most. Once he
signs, each trait gets its own estimate on his card.

## Signing

- Signing uses one **pick**. Amateur scouting gives 2 to 6 free picks a season, and up to 4 more can
  be bought with gold pucks.
- Signing costs gold pucks, and every further signing in a season costs more than the one before.
- A junior can only be signed where the squad has room at his position.
- Juniors can be signed through day 7. After that their real ratings stay visible through day 14,
  but they can no longer be signed, and they leave at the end of the season.
- A signed junior joins the squad with his real ratings revealed.

## Consolation prize

If your first team's season ends in the group stage, your To sign juniors get ten Basic and the
rest Advanced narrowings for free; a team knocked out in the quarterfinal gets a Basic narrowing for
each. These go past the usual floor.
