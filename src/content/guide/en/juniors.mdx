At seventeen a prospect is only a range of possibilities. Scouting is how you find out what he
really is before you sign him.

## Two seasons per junior

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="event_repeat" title="Scout at 17, sign at 18">
    Prospects are seventeen: this season you only scout them. At eighteen they move to **To sign**
    and can be signed early in the next season.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="groups" title="Fifteen a season">
    Your scouts find fifteen prospects every season. How many you may sign is the number of your
    picks.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

<Callout type="note" title="Why it is not a draft like the NHL's">
  In the NHL young players are chosen at the draft: once a year, on a fixed day, seven rounds, with
  teams taking turns in reverse order of the standings and picking from one shared pool of players aged at least
  18 ([NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/devils/team/draft), [Hockey Answered](https://hockeyanswered.com/how-the-nhl-entry-draft-works-a-complete-guide/)). Only the team that drafted a player may
  sign him. Europe has no draft: junior teams belong to professional clubs and develop their players
  ([International Hockey Wiki](https://internationalhockeywiki.com/ihw/index.php/Junior_hockey_in_Europe)).

  Ice Flow is a compromise between the two:

  - **from the NHL** come signing at 18, a limited number of picks and an advantage for a team that
    went out early (see Consolation prize below),
  - **from Europe** comes that the prospects are yours alone - fifteen from your own junior team, with
    nobody to compete with for them,
  - and the choice is made **offline**: it does not happen on a set day and hour. You sign any time
    until day 7 of the season, so nobody has to be at their phone at a given moment or wait for the
    clubs ahead of them to choose.
</Callout>

## Getting a clearer picture

A prospect's abilities and traits are shown as **ranges**. The real value lies somewhere inside.

<Screenshot name="juniors" alt="Prospects list with rating ranges and the TRT arrows" caption="Prospects: every rating as a range and TRT as two arrows. Scout narrows them." />

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="today" title="Day by day">
    The range narrows by itself a little every day of the season.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="description" title="Scouting reports">
    A report bought with gold pucks narrows it faster - Basic a little, Advanced more, Deep the most.
    Better amateur scouting staff make every report sharper.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="vertical_align_bottom" title="A floor">
    A bought report cannot narrow the range below a floor, and the floor is lower the fewer days of
    the season are left.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="lock" title="Fixed at eighteen">
    At eighteen the range stays what it was at the end of the scouting season. Waiting during the
    signing window does not improve it.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="unfold_more" title="TRT">
    A junior's traits - talent, professionalism, injury resistance, discipline and mentality - in one
    estimate, with talent and professionalism weighing the most. After signing, each trait gets its
    own estimate on his card.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Signing

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="confirmation_number" title="Picks">
    Signing uses one pick. Amateur scouting gives 2 to 6 free picks a season; up to 4 more can be
    bought with gold pucks.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="payments" title="Price">
    Signing costs gold pucks, and every further signing in a season costs more than the one before.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="groups" title="Room in the squad">
    A junior can only be signed where the squad has room at his position.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="schedule" title="Signing window">
    Through day 7. After that, real ratings stay visible through day 14, but nobody can be signed,
    and unsigned juniors leave at the end of the season.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="visibility" title="The reveal">
    A signed junior joins the squad with his real ratings revealed.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Consolation prize

If your first team's season ends in the group stage, your To sign juniors get ten Basic and the
rest Advanced narrowings for free. A team knocked out in the quarterfinal gets one Basic narrowing
each. These go past the usual floor.

It is the idea of the NHL draft lottery, in which the teams that missed the playoff draw for the top
picks ([NHL.com](https://www.nhl.com/devils/team/draft)). Since nobody in Ice Flow competes for your prospects, a team gets no
better position but a clearer picture of the juniors it can sign.
