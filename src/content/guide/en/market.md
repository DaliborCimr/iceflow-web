## Buying

Everything on the market is paid in gold pucks.

1. **Search.** Choose filters and search; the market offers one player at a time, with a short wait
   before the next search.
2. **Held for you.** The player found is held for you for 30 seconds - nobody else is offered him
   meanwhile.
3. **Bid or buy.** Bid at least the next minimum bid, or buy him at once for the quick-buy price.
   Your gold pucks are held while your bid leads and come back if you are outbid.

A listing has no countdown until its first bid. The first bid starts an **eight-minute auction** in
real time; the highest bid at closing buys the player. A bid with less than 90 seconds left extends
the auction so that 90 seconds remain.

You can only bid where your squad has room at the position, and the place counts as taken from the
moment you bid.

## Selling

- **Auction.** Move the player to the reserves, then list him with an asking price between 80 % and
  110 % of his value. A listed player plays for neither of your teams. A listing can be taken off
  only while nobody has bid.
- **Quick sell.** Closes at once at 70 % of the player's reference value.
- The selling club receives **75 %** of the sale price - for a quick sale that is about 52.5 % of the
  reference value.
- Selling may not take a position below 5 skaters or 3 goalies. Players in the last season of their
  contract and players who have announced retirement can be sold only through day 15.

The market is open all season until the evening of the last day and reopens with the new season.

## Contracts

- The seasons left on a contract count down at the end of every season. A contract that runs out
  without an extension lets the player leave as a free agent.
- A contract can be extended at any time once it has two seasons or fewer left. The resulting
  contract may have at most five seasons in total.
- The salary for added seasons depends on the player's ratings, age and expected development over
  the contract, and the signing bonus for those seasons is paid at once. Front-office staff reduce
  the salary and bonus of new contracts by up to 12 %.
- A player who has announced retirement cannot be extended and leaves at the end of the season.

## Squad size

A club holds at most 9 players at each skater position and 5 goalies, 44 in all. If a position falls
short, the club fills it automatically - to 4 skaters and 2 goalies in the morning and to 5 and 3 in
the afternoon - paying from available gold pucks. Injured players still count toward these numbers.
