Everything on the transfer market is paid in **gold pucks**, and auctions run in real time.

## Buying a player

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="filter_alt" title="1. Search">
    Choose filters and search. The market offers one player at a time, with a short wait before the
    next search.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="timer" title="2. Held for you">
    The player found is held for you for 30 seconds - nobody else is offered him meanwhile.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="gavel" title="3. Bid or buy">
    Bid at least the next minimum bid, or buy him at once for the quick-buy price. Your gold pucks
    are held while your bid leads and come back if you are outbid.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

### How an auction ends

A listing has no countdown until someone bids. The **first bid starts an eight-minute auction**, and
the highest bid at closing buys the player. A bid in the last 90 seconds extends the auction so that
90 seconds remain.

<Callout type="note">
  You can only bid where your squad has room at the position, and the place counts as taken from the
  moment you bid.
</Callout>

## Selling a player

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="sell" title="Auction">
    Move the player to the reserves, then list him with an asking price between 80 % and 110 % of
    his value. A club can have at most three players listed at a time. A listed player plays for neither of your teams. You can take him off only while
    nobody has bid.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="flash_on" title="Quick sell">
    Closes at once at 70 % of the player's reference value. It cannot be undone.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="percent" title="What you receive">
    The selling club receives 75 % of the sale price - for a quick sale that is about 52.5 % of the
    reference value.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="block" title="Limits">
    Selling may not take a position below 5 skaters or 3 goalies. Players in the last season of
    their contract and players who have announced retirement can be sold only through day 15. Once a
    contract is extended, the player is no longer in his last season and can be sold later too.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

The market is open all season until the evening of the last day and reopens with the new season.

## Contracts

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="hourglass_bottom" title="Length">
    The seasons left on a contract count down at the end of every season. A contract that runs out
    without an extension lets the player leave as a free agent.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="update" title="Extension">
    Possible at any time once two seasons or fewer are left. The resulting contract may have at most
    five seasons in total, not five added ones.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="payments" title="Salary">
    The salary for added seasons depends on the player's ratings, age and expected development over
    the contract. The signing bonus for those seasons is paid at once. Front-office staff cut the
    salary and bonus of new contracts by up to 12 %.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="logout" title="Retirement">
    A player who has announced retirement cannot be extended and leaves at the end of the season.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Squad size

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="groups" title="Limits">
    At most 9 players at each skater position and 5 goalies, 44 in all.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="autorenew" title="Automatic top-up">
    During the season, selling can never take a position below 5 skaters or 3 goalies. A squad
    typically drops below that on the first day of a new season, when players who retired and players
    whose contracts ran out leave automatically. The club then fills the gaps itself - to 4 skaters
    and 2 goalies in the morning, so a lineup can be played, and to 5 and 3 in the afternoon - paying
    from available gold pucks. In the morning it tells you which positions the afternoon top-up will
    fill, so you can sign your own players in between. Injured players still count.
  </Rule>
</Rules>
