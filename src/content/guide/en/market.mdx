Everything on the transfer market is paid in **gold pucks**, and auctions run in real time.

## Buying a player

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="filter_alt" title="1. Search">
    Choose filters and search - the market deals you up to five players. You can search again after
    5 seconds, plus 5 seconds for each player found.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="timer" title="2. Your search cards">
    The cards are not yours alone: other managers can bid on the same players. They last 2 minutes
    for each player found (10 minutes at most) or until you search again; until then each card shows
    its new price, or that the player has left the market. A minute after you close the app, your
    search is gone. A card opens like a player card in your team - all attributes including PHY and
    EXP - but without statistics; its middle tile shows the bid history.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="gavel" title="3. Bid or buy">
    Bid at least the next minimum bid, or buy him at once for the quick-buy price. Your gold pucks
    are held while your bid leads and come back if you are outbid.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

### How an auction ends

A listing has no countdown until someone bids. The **first bid starts an eight-minute auction**, and
the highest bid at closing buys the player. A bid in the last 90 seconds extends the auction so that
90 seconds remain. <TestOnly />

<Callout type="note">
  You can only bid where your squad has room at the position, and the place counts as taken from the
  moment you bid.
</Callout>

## Selling a player

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="sell" title="Auction">
    Move the player to the reserves, then list him with an asking price between 80 % and 110 % of
    his value. A listed player plays for neither of your teams. You can take him off only while
    nobody has bid.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="flash_on" title="Quick sell">
    Closes at once at 70 % of the player's reference value and cannot be undone. Ending the contract
    early costs a buyout in Flow, paid at once: two thirds of what the contract still owes - the rest
    of this season and every later season - or one third for a player under 26. For a player who has
    announced retirement only this season counts. Without enough Flow for the buyout the quick sell
    is refused. An auction sale pays no buyout.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="percent" title="What you receive">
    The selling club receives 75 % of the sale price - for a quick sale that is about 52.5 % of the
    reference value.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="block" title="Limits">
    Selling may not take a position below 5 skaters or 3 goalies. Players in the last season of
    their contract and players who have announced retirement can be sold only through day 15. Once a
    contract is extended, the player is no longer in his last season and can be sold later too.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

The market is open all season until the evening of the last day and reopens with the new season.

<Callout type="note" title="Where the buyout comes from">
  The share is the NHL's: two thirds of the remaining salary, one third for a player under 26
  ([PuckPedia](https://www.puckpedia.com/salary-cap/buyouts)). The NHL spreads it over twice the remaining term because of the salary
  cap. Ice Flow has no salary cap, so the buyout is paid at once.
</Callout>

## Contracts

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="hourglass_bottom" title="Length">
    The seasons left on a contract count down at the end of every season. A contract that runs out
    without an extension lets the player leave as a free agent.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="update" title="Extension">
    Possible at any time once two seasons or fewer are left. The resulting contract may have at most
    five seasons in total, not five added ones.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="payments" title="Base salary">
    The base salary for added seasons depends on the player's ratings, age and expected development
    over the contract. Front-office staff cut the salary and bonus of new contracts by up to 12 %.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="handshake" title="Negotiation">
    From your club's second season you name the salary yourself - as a share of the base salary - and
    the length. A low offer is refused and a generous one accepted; in between it depends on the
    player's ambition and loyalty, the length, your club's strength and success and your front
    office. The signing bonus follows the offered salary and the added seasons and is paid once the
    deal is accepted.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="replay" title="Attempts">
    Each offer uses an attempt: five for your own player, three for a free agent, whom you can sign
    only after agreeing a contract. Once they run out, you cannot negotiate with that player again
    this season.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="logout" title="Retirement">
    A player who has announced retirement cannot be extended and leaves at the end of the season.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Squad size

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="groups" title="Limits">
    At most 9 players at each skater position and 5 goalies, 44 in all.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="autorenew" title="Automatic top-up">
    During the season, selling can never take a position below 5 skaters or 3 goalies. A squad
    typically drops below that on the first day of a new season, when players who retired and players
    whose contracts ran out leave automatically. The club then fills the gaps itself - to 4 skaters
    and 2 goalies in the morning, so a lineup can be played, and to 5 and 3 in the afternoon - paying
    from available gold pucks. In the morning it tells you which positions the afternoon top-up will
    fill, so you can sign your own players in between. Injured players still count.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

<Callout type="note" title="Why 44 players">
  The squad carries two full teams - the first team and the farm, each with four units of five and
  two goalies. That is 8 players at each skater position and 4 goalies, 44 in all. The limit per
  position is one higher so that you can buy the better player before selling the worse one.
</Callout>
