IceFlow does not copy one rulebook. Where the NHL and the IIHF differ, the game picks the rule that
fits a short online season better, and a few rules are its own. This page lists them and says where
each one comes from.

## Game length and overtime

| Rule | In IceFlow | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Regulation | Three 20-minute periods | NHL and IIHF |
| Overtime in the group stage | One sudden-death period of 5 minutes, 3 skaters a side | NHL; IIHF uses the same in its preliminary rounds (IIHF rule 84.1) |
| Penalty in overtime | The other side gets a skater: 3 on 3 becomes 4 on 3, two penalties 5 on 3; a team never drops below three skaters | NHL and IIHF (IIHF rule 84.3) |
| Shootout | Five shooters a side, then sudden death | IIHF (rule 84.4); the NHL uses three |
| Overtime in knockout games | After an intermission, 20-minute sudden-death periods 3 on 3 until a goal; a shootout only if three overtimes end level, which is rare | Combined: period length from the NHL playoffs (played 5 on 5 there), 3 on 3 from the IIHF, whose knockout overtime lasts 10 minutes (rule 84.5) |

## Penalties

| Rule | In IceFlow | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Delayed penalty | Play goes on until the penalised side touches the puck; the other side may pull its goalie for an extra skater | NHL and IIHF (IIHF rule 15.1) |
| Power-play goal | Ends the minor penalty with the least time left; a short-handed goal ends nothing | NHL and IIHF (IIHF rule 16.2) |
| Coincidental minors at full strength | Both sides play 4 on 4 | NHL; the IIHF substitutes both players and stays 5 on 5 (rule 19.1) |
| Third penalty | A side never has fewer than three skaters; a further penalty waits until one runs out | NHL and IIHF (IIHF rule 26.1) |
| Fighting | 5-minute major for both players | NHL; the IIHF adds a game misconduct (rule 46) |
| Major penalty | 5 minutes and a game misconduct | NHL and IIHF (IIHF rule 20) |

## Table and competitions

| Rule | In IceFlow | Comes from |
|---|---|---|
| Points | Regulation win 3, overtime or shootout win 2, overtime or shootout loss 1 | IIHF competitions; the NHL gives 2 for every win |
| Equal points | Goal difference, then games between the tied teams, then goals scored | IceFlow |
| League playoff | Top eight; quarterfinal to two wins, semifinal and final one game each, the better-ranked team at home | IceFlow - shaped by the 21-day season |
| Elite, farm and Rapid | Every knockout round is one game | IceFlow |

## What is not modelled as a separate rule

The match is a simulation of play, not of every whistle. Icing, offside and similar stoppages are
part of how the simulation moves the puck around; they are not counted or called as separate events.
Video review, coach's challenges and penalty shots during play are not part of the game.
