Ice Flow does not copy one rulebook. Where the NHL and the IIHF differ, the game picks the rule that
suits a short online season, and a few rules are its own. Each rule below says where it comes from.

<Callout type="note">
  Every rule links to where it comes from: IIHF rule numbers open the article in the IIHF Official
  Rule Book 2026/27, NHL links open the NHL Official Rules. Rules marked **Ice Flow** are the game's
  own and are described in the in-app help.
</Callout>

## Game length and overtime

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="timer" title="Regulation: 3 × 20 minutes">
    As in the NHL and the IIHF.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="more_time" title="Overtime in the group stage: 5 minutes, 3 on 3">
    Sudden death. **[NHL](https://media.nhl.com/site/asset/public/ext/2025-26/2025-26Rules.pdf)**; the IIHF plays the same in its preliminary rounds ([rule 84.1](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/84)).
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="person_add" title="A penalty in overtime adds a skater">
    3 on 3 becomes 4 on 3, two penalties make it 5 on 3. A team never drops below three skaters.
    **[NHL](https://media.nhl.com/site/asset/public/ext/2025-26/2025-26Rules.pdf) and IIHF** ([rule 84.3](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/84)).
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="sports_hockey" title="Shootout: five shooters">
    Then sudden death. **IIHF** ([rule 84.4](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/84)); the NHL uses three.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="all_inclusive" title="Knockout overtime: 20-minute periods, 3 on 3, until a goal">
    After an intermission. A shootout only if three overtimes end level, which is rare. **A
    combination**: the period length comes from the NHL playoff (played 5 on 5 there), the 3 on 3
    from the IIHF, whose knockout overtime lasts 10 minutes ([rule 84.5](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/84)).
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Penalties

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="front_hand" title="Delayed penalty">
    Play goes on until the penalised team touches the puck; the other team may pull its goalie for
    an extra skater. **[NHL](https://media.nhl.com/site/asset/public/ext/2025-26/2025-26Rules.pdf) and IIHF** ([rule 15.1](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/15)).
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="sports_score" title="A power-play goal ends the penalty">
    The minor with the least time left ends. A short-handed goal ends nothing. **[NHL](https://media.nhl.com/site/asset/public/ext/2025-26/2025-26Rules.pdf) and IIHF** (rule
    16.2).
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="compare_arrows" title="Coincidental minors at full strength: 4 on 4">
    **[NHL](https://media.nhl.com/site/asset/public/ext/2025-26/2025-26Rules.pdf)**. The IIHF substitutes both players at once and stays 5 on 5 ([rule 19.1](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/19)).
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="group_remove" title="Never fewer than three skaters">
    A further penalty waits until one of the running ones ends. **[NHL](https://media.nhl.com/site/asset/public/ext/2025-26/2025-26Rules.pdf) and IIHF** ([rule 26.1](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/26)).
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="sports_mma" title="Fighting: 5 minutes">
    A major for both players. **[NHL](https://media.nhl.com/site/asset/public/ext/2025-26/2025-26Rules.pdf)**; the IIHF adds a game misconduct ([rule 46](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/46)).
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="block" title="Major penalty: 5 minutes and out of the game">
    **[NHL](https://media.nhl.com/site/asset/public/ext/2025-26/2025-26Rules.pdf) and IIHF** ([rule 20](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/rule-book-2026-27/20)).
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Table and competitions

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="leaderboard" title="Points 3-2-1">
    3 for a win in regulation, 2 for a win in overtime or the shootout, 1 for such a loss.
    **IIHF competitions** ([IIHF Event Code, art. 196](https://test.rulebook.iihf.com/documents/event-code-women-and-junior/196)); the NHL gives 2 points for every win.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="format_list_numbered" title="Equal points">
    Goal difference, then games between the tied teams, then goals scored. **Ice Flow**.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="account_tree" title="Playoff formats">
    League: top eight, quarterfinal to two wins, semifinal and final one game each. Elite, farm and
    Rapid: every knockout round one game. **Ice Flow** - built around the 21-day season.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## What is not a separate rule

The match simulates play, not every whistle. Icing, offside and similar stoppages are part of how
the simulation moves the puck; they are not called or counted as separate events. Video review,
coach's challenges and penalty shots during play are not part of the game.
