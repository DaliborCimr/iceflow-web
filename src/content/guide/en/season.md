## A season in 21 days

A season lasts 21 real days. Each game day has three parts - morning, afternoon and evening - so a
season has 63 blocks. Games, training sessions, sponsor payments and the market all run on this
rhythm. Teams play their league games in fixed blocks; between them you train, trade and plan.

## Leagues drawn by strength

Before every season clubs are drawn into twelve-team leagues. The draw looks at:

- the team's strength over the previous season,
- where it finished,
- how its results compared with its strength,
- how active its manager was.

The aim is a league of teams that can beat each other. Places that managers do not fill are taken
by bot clubs, which play, train and trade by the same rules.

## Group stage and playoff

Everyone meets everyone four times, twice at home - 44 games. A regulation win is worth 3 points,
an overtime or shootout win 2 and an overtime or shootout loss 1. Teams level on points are split by
goal difference, then by their games against each other, then by goals scored.

The top eight go to the playoff. The quarterfinal on day 20 is a series to two wins; the semifinal
and the final on day 21 are one game each. The better-ranked team plays at home. Because the
playoff fits into the last two days, a team knocked out does not wait a week for the season to end.

## Elite

Elite is a cup of the best clubs, drawn for the next season in full groups of 24. Managed clubs that
reached the semifinal of their league come first; free places then go to fifth-placed managed clubs
and to permanent bot clubs of similar strength. If there are not enough eligible clubs for a full
group, there is no Elite group.

Elite has six group rounds, each against a club with a similar record and never the same one twice.
The top sixteen play a knockout of one-game rounds. Elite shares the league calendar: in some
afternoon slots it replaces the league round, and clubs without an Elite game get two team training
sessions instead.

## Farm

Your farm team plays at the same time as the first team, in its own league with its own table and
playoff (top eight, all rounds one game). Whoever the first team does not dress plays for the farm.
Farm results never mix with the first team's, and they do not qualify for Elite.

## Rapid league

The Rapid league is a quick four-team tournament outside the season. One entry costs one ad (none
with the ad-free pass), at most ten a day. Teams of similar strength are grouped together; if four do
not gather within a few minutes, bots fill the league. All six games are played at once, without
home ice.

Your current lineup and tactics play, but nothing from the season counts: everyone is equally
rested, form and mentality play no part, and nothing carries over - no fatigue, injuries, statistics
or experience. Places one to four pay 5, 3, 2 and 1 stars. The Rapid league never overlaps your own
match and is closed between the last block of a season and the start of the next one.
