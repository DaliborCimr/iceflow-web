A season in Ice Flow lasts three weeks of real time. It is short on purpose: every day matters, and
nobody waits long for the next chance.

## The season at a glance

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="calendar_month" title="21 game days">
    One game day is one real day. The season starts on day 1 and the final is played on day 21.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="schedule" title="Three parts of the day">
    Morning, afternoon and evening. That makes 63 blocks a season - games, training sessions,
    sponsor payments and the market all run on them.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="shield" title="Your league">
    Twelve teams. Everyone meets everyone four times, twice at home - 44 games in the group stage.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="trophy" title="What you play for">
    First place in the league group stage, the league playoff title and the Elite title.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Leagues drawn by strength

Before every season clubs are drawn into new leagues, so that you play teams you can beat and that
can beat you. The draw looks at:

- the team's strength over the previous season,
- where it finished,
- how its results compared with its strength,
- how active its manager was.

Places that managers do not fill are taken by **bot clubs**. They play, train and trade by the same
rules as everyone else.

## Points and the table

| Result | Points |
|---|---|
| Win in regulation | 3 |
| Win in overtime or shootout | 2 |
| Loss in overtime or shootout | 1 |
| Loss in regulation | 0 |

Teams level on points are split by goal difference, then by their games against each other, then by
goals scored. The line in the table marks the last playoff place.

## Playoff

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="looks_one" title="Top eight">
    The first eight teams of the group stage play the playoff.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="event" title="Day 20: quarterfinal">
    A series to two wins.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="trophy" title="Day 21: semifinal and final">
    One game each. The better-ranked team always plays at home.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

<Callout type="note" title="Why the playoff is this short">
  The quarterfinal is played to two wins because reaching the league semifinal decides a place in
  next season's Elite - a single game would decide that too much by chance. The semifinal and final
  are one game each so that the whole playoff fits into the last two days. A team that is knocked out
  therefore never waits more than two days for the new season.
</Callout>

## Elite

Elite is the league of the best clubs, played alongside your own league.

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="how_to_reg" title="Who qualifies">
    Every managed club that reached the semifinal of its league gets a place. Elite is drawn for the
    next season in groups of 24, and the free places are filled in this order: fifth-placed managed
    clubs, then permanent bot clubs that reached a semifinal in their own league - those closest in
    strength to the managed clubs first - and only then other managed clubs by their table. There are
    always enough permanent bots, so a semifinalist never misses out.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="grid_view" title="Group stage: 6 Swiss-system rounds">
    Each round against a club with a similar record and never the same one twice - the
    [Swiss system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss-system_tournament). In the current calendar the rounds are played on days 2, 4, 6, 9, 11
    and 13.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="account_tree" title="Playoff">
    The top sixteen play on, one game per round, the better-ranked club at home: round of sixteen on
    day 15, quarterfinal on day 16, semifinal on day 18 and final on day 19 - before the league
    playoff.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="calendar_month" title="Part of the season calendar">
    Elite has its own blocks in the season calendar. Clubs that do not play in an Elite block get an extra
    team training session in that block.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Farm

Your farm team plays at the same time as the first team, in its own league with its own table and
playoff (top eight, every round one game). Farm results never mix with the first team's and do not
qualify for Elite.

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="groups" title="Who plays for the farm">
    Whoever the first team does not dress - you never pick the farm's lineup, only who is left out.
    You can keep a player out of both games, for instance so that he trains individually instead.
    A player listed on the transfer market plays for neither team.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="person_add" title="Borrowed players">
    Where the farm is short at a position, it borrows make-weight players who are clearly weaker
    than its league. A wider squad means your own young players play instead - and develop.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="trending_up" title="Development">
    A farm game develops the players who play it, but less than the same game in the first team
    would.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

### Who may play in a playoff

A playoff place is earned in the group stage: a player needs **six group-stage games**. The same
rule applies to the first team's playoff and to the farm's, but the games count differently.

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="sports_hockey" title="First team">
    Every group-stage game counts, farm games included - a player who spent the season on the farm
    may play in the first team's playoff.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="shield" title="Farm">
    Only farm games count. A first-team game never counts towards the farm's six, so the farm cannot
    be reinforced from above for its playoff.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="healing" title="Injury">
    A game missed through injury counts as played - for the block he was injured in, not for a game
    earlier that day he could have played.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="rule" title="Two exceptions">
    Each team may still dress two players who have not qualified, per game. In the first team they go
    to the goalies first; past the two, an ineligible player's place goes to the best eligible
    reserve, or the line plays a man short. On the farm they go first to positions it would
    otherwise have to borrow for, then to the best player left.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

The farm screen shows each player's count, for example 4/6. More in [Training](../training/) and
[Players](../player/).

## Rapid league

The Rapid league is a quick four-team tournament for Stars that sits outside the season calendar.

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="smart_display" title="Entry">
    One ad per entry (none with the Ads Pass), up to ten a day. Teams of similar strength are grouped
    together; if four do not gather within a few minutes, bots fill the league. <TestOnly />
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="bolt" title="Played at once">
    All six games are played immediately, everyone against everyone, without home ice. <TestOnly />
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="balance" title="Nothing from the season counts">
    Your current lineup and tactics play, but everyone is equally rested, form and mentality play no
    part and nothing carries over - no fatigue, injuries, statistics or experience. Only each
    player's day remains.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="stars" title="Stars">
    Places one to four pay 5, 3, 2 and 1 stars. Bots get nothing for their placing.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

The Rapid league never overlaps your own match, and it is closed between the last block of a season
and the start of the next one.
