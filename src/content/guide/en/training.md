## Sessions through the day

New sessions arrive whenever a part of the day begins - morning, afternoon or evening.

- **Team sessions.** Two in a part of the day in which neither your first team nor your farm plays,
  one when either of them plays. A team session trains the whole squad at once, each player at his
  own intensity.
- **Individual sessions.** Every player gets one per part of the day, and a second one when your
  club played in the part just ended and he spent no time on the ice - a goalie who sat on the bench
  included. An individual session trains one attribute you choose.
- A session can be used in the part of the day it arrives in and in the next one; after that it
  lapses and counts as rest.

## Focus and intensity

A team session has a focus: Balanced, Technical or Physical. Each player then trains at one of three
intensities:

| Intensity | Development | Fatigue |
|---|---|---|
| Hard | most | +5 |
| Light | less | -5 |
| Rest | none | -15 |

The app suggests an intensity from the player's fatigue - Hard below 6, Light from 6, Rest from 16 -
and you can change it. A player whose fatigue is above 35 when a part of the day begins spends his
carried-over individual sessions on rest automatically. A fit, resilient and professional player
recovers a little more.

## Boost

A boost spent on a player in a session takes 10 more fatigue off, so he can train harder. It only
helps a player with fatigue of 6 or more. Boosts are earned by watching an ad, up to 10 a day, and
unused ones are kept.

## Fit colours and potential

The colour of an attribute shows how it sits for the player's type: blue is low for his type and
trains faster, green is in line, orange is already high and trains slowly, red is far above his type
and barely moves.

Every player also has a ceiling set by his talent. The closer he gets to it, the less each session
adds; better training facilities raise it a little.

## Staff

Coaches and training facilities improve technical and physical training; medical staff also help
physical training and recovery. Player-development staff improve what players gain from farm games,
not from training. An injured player does not train.
