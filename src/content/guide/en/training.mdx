Training is where players grow between games. The art is in balancing development against fatigue:
a player who trains hard every session will be tired when it matters.

## When sessions arrive

New sessions arrive whenever a part of the day begins - morning, afternoon and evening.

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="groups" title="Team sessions">
    Two in a part of the day when neither your first team nor your farm plays, one when either does.
    A team session trains the whole squad at once, each player at his own intensity.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="person_search" title="Individual sessions">
    One per player per part of the day, aimed at the attribute you choose. A second one when your
    club played in the part just ended and he spent no time on the ice - a goalie on the bench
    included. A newly bought player gets one at once.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="hourglass_bottom" title="Use them in time">
    A session can be used in the part of the day it arrives in and in the next one. After that it
    lapses and counts as rest.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## How hard to train

| Intensity | Development | Fatigue |
|---|---|---|
| Hard | most | +5 |
| Light | less | -5 |
| Rest | none | -15 |

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="lightbulb" title="Suggested intensity">
    The app suggests one from the player's fatigue - Hard below 6, Light from 6, Rest from 16. You
    can change it.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="hotel" title="Very tired players rest on their own">
    When a part of the day begins and a player's fatigue is above 35, his carried-over individual
    sessions are spent on rest until he is back around 35.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="smart_display" title="Boost">
    A boost takes 10 more fatigue off in a session, so he can train harder. It helps only a player
    with fatigue of 6 or more. One boost per ad, up to 10 a day; unused boosts are kept.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

A team session also has a focus: **Balanced**, **Technical** or **Physical**.

<Callout type="tip">
  A fit, resilient and professional player recovers a little more from every session.
</Callout>

## Why some attributes grow faster

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="palette" title="Fit colours">
    The colour of an attribute shows how it sits for the player's type. **Blue** is low for his type
    and trains faster, **green** is in line, **orange** is already high and trains slowly, **red**
    is far above his type and barely moves.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="show_chart" title="Potential">
    Every player has a ceiling set by his talent. The closer he gets to it, the less each session
    adds. Better training facilities raise it a little.
  </Rule>
</Rules>

## Staff

<Rules>
  <Rule icon="school" title="Who helps">
    Coaches and training facilities improve technical and physical training; medical staff also help
    physical training and recovery. Player-development staff improve what players gain from farm
    games, not from training.
  </Rule>
  <Rule icon="local_hospital" title="Injured players">
    An injured player does not train, and sessions he was given while injured are not his to use
    once he is fit again.
  </Rule>
</Rules>
