## 2. What data we process
Google account. You sign in with your Google account. Google gives us the account's identifier, your e-mail address (verified ones only) and a link to your profile picture; we refresh the e-mail and the link at every sign-in. We also store when the account was created and when you last signed in. We store no passwords – the game has none.

Game profile. Your club's name, your manager nickname (up to 20 characters), your club's nationality (a game setting, not your real citizenship), the club's crest and colours, and everything the game creates: squad, matches, finances, challenges, purchases with in-game stars and in-game news.

Device. While notifications are on, we store the token Firebase Cloud Messaging delivers them to and the device platform.

Technical data. After sign-in you get a random access token valid for 24 hours. The server's operating logs hold a request identifier, the club identifier, the request's address, its result and any error, so we can trace a problem you report. We use IP addresses only on the interface for computer-run clubs, where we keep one for 5 minutes to stop password guessing. <mark class="todo">TO COMPLETE: what the hosting and CDN log, IP addresses in particular</mark>.

On your device. The app keeps the access token, your club registration details, the language and your settings (sounds, notifications, appearance) on the phone.

What we do not collect: no analytics or crash reporting, no advertising identifier, location, contacts, photos or payment data. The game has no ads and no real-money purchases yet; if they arrive, we will update this policy in time.

## 3. Why we use your data and on what basis
- Account, sign-in and the game – performance of a contract (the game's terms), Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.
- Notifications – your consent, Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. You give it in the phone's system prompt and can withdraw it at any time in the game's Settings or the phone's settings.
- Security and fixing errors – our legitimate interest in a secure, working service, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

## 4. Who receives your data
- Google LLC – Google sign-in and notification delivery (Firebase Cloud Messaging). The app loads your profile picture straight from Google's servers. Google may process data in the USA; the transfer relies on the Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
- <mark class="todo">HOSTING, DATABASE AND CDN PROVIDER, COUNTRY</mark> – running the server and storing the data.
- Other players see your club's name, crest and colours (tables, matches, transfers).
We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

- Notification token: until you turn notifications off, or 30 days after the device was last used.
